### Robot Simulation.

#### 1. Directions
- North : (0,1)
- South : (0,-1)
- West  : (-1,0)
- East  : (1,0)

#### 2. To Turn 90 Degree Left
- Do (x,y) --> (-y,x)

#### 3. To Turn 90 Degree Right
- Do (x,y) --> (y,-x)
